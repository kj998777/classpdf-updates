FAQ

Where is my ink saved? Does my original PDF change? Your original PDF is not changed. Ink is saved separately inside the app's folder (a hidden .classpdf folder on iPad — please don't delete it).

What's free? Every feature is free for your first 7 days. After that, writing, tape covers and scanned-text search stay free; auto label cover, tape review and audio notes keep a small free preview; Class mode and problem finding need a plan. Study, Class or Both are one-time purchases — not subscriptions.

I bought on another iPad, or reinstalled the app. Sign in with the same Apple ID and tap Settings › Plan › Restore purchases. You won't be charged again.

How do I get a refund? App Store purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can I bring notes from other apps? Yes. Library › Import accepts PDFs and other note-app files (.goodnotes, .note). Ink comes in as editable strokes, with recordings when available.

How do I back up? Library › ⋯ › Auto backup: choose a folder (iCloud Drive, Google Drive…). PDFs, ink and recordings are copied there — only what changed — when you leave the app and every 10 minutes. To restore, Library › ⋯ › Restore from backup.

Can I use it in Korean? Yes. It follows your device language; change it in Settings › Display › Language (Auto / 한국어 / English).

Recording doesn't pick up sound. Allow the microphone in iPad Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone › Inkover.

Is anything sent anywhere? No. There are no servers, analytics or crash reports. See the Privacy Policy.
