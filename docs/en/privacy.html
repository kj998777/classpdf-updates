Inkover Privacy Policy

In short

Inkover has no accounts, no servers, and no analytics or crash reporting. Your PDFs, ink and recordings are stored only on your device and in a cloud folder you choose yourself.

1. Information we collect

None. The operator receives no personal information.

  • There is no sign-up or sign-in.
  • There are no ads, analytics tools, or crash reports.
  • The iPad app declares "No tracking, no data collected" to Apple (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy).

2. What is stored on your device

To work, the app keeps the following on your device only:

  • Ink, covers, tape and notes for each PDF (kept separately — your original PDF is not modified unless you choose to save page edits)
  • Audio notes, class recordings and photos you add
  • Recent documents, settings, thumbnails, and recognized text from scanned pages (cache)
  • On Mac only: an activity log (main.log) with file names and paths. It never leaves your Mac unless you send it to us for support — please check it first.

3. Internet access

The app connects to the internet only in these cases:

  1. Update check (Mac only) — downloads version information and updates from GitHub. No data is sent by the app; GitHub may see your IP address as with any download.
  2. A cloud folder you choose — if you turn on sync, backup or import and pick a folder (iCloud Drive, Google Drive, Dropbox…), the app reads and writes files in that folder. Uploading is done by that cloud provider's app under its own privacy policy. Inkover never signs in to your cloud account.
  3. Printing (Mac) — talks to the printer you choose through macOS printing.
  4. App Store purchases (iPad) — see section 4.

The iPad app does not otherwise connect to the internet. Text recognition and transcription run on the device.

4. In-app purchases and free trial (iPad)

  • Payments are handled entirely by Apple (App Store). Neither the app nor the operator receives your payment details.
  • The app asks Apple's StoreKit on your device which plans your Apple ID owns. This is not sent to the operator.
  • The 7-day free trial is calculated from the first-launch date stored on your device only.
  • The operator can see only Apple's aggregated, non-identifying sales reports in App Store Connect.

5. Device permissions

You'll be asked the first time a feature needs them; everything else keeps working if you decline.

Permission Used for
Microphone Class recording, audio notes
Camera (iPad) Taking a photo to insert into a page
Photo library (iPad) Choosing a photo to insert into a page
Downloads / Desktop / Documents folders (Mac) Opening or collecting PDFs in those folders
Automation — Preview (Mac) Opening Preview's print dialog

Recordings and photos are stored only on the device (or in the folder you chose).

6. Students' information

Inkover does not collect or manage student information. If your PDFs, file names or ink contain student names or scores, they are stored the same way — only on your device and in your chosen folder. Be careful when sharing that cloud folder with others.

7. Deleting your data

  • iPad: deleting the app removes its folder (PDFs, ink, recordings). Export or back up anything you need first.
  • Mac: delete ~/Library/Application Support/ClassPDF to remove all ink and settings.
  • Copies in a sync or backup folder are removed from that cloud folder directly.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page with the date and what changed.
